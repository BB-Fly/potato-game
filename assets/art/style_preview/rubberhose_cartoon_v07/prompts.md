# Rubberhose Cartoon v07 Prompts

## Shared Style

Bright hand-drawn vintage rubber-hose cartoon, close to Cuphead forest enemy and stage prop sprites: thick black ink outline, slightly wobbly hand-drawn contours, flat cel colors, playful theatrical expression, low-to-medium detail suitable for 2D game animation.

Avoid realistic rendering, 3D, anime mascot, western RPG, dark fantasy, gritty horror, over-detailed concept art, glossy AI rendering, complex backgrounds, readable text, and watermarks.

## Hero Body

Generate one potato protagonist body sprite only. The character should feel like a 30-year-old wandering martial hero: experienced, calm, slightly weathered, not young, not overly cute, not slim humanoid. Use a small oval potato body with natural uneven potato bumps, subtle freckles/scars, thick expressive eyebrows, steady determined eyes, a short red scarf, one tiny green sprout, and two little brown shoes. No arms, no hands, no weapon.

Use a perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background. Centered 3/4 front view, full body, generous padding, clean isolated sprite. Four floating hand sprites will be animated separately later, so do not include arms, hands, or held items. Make the outline less perfectly smooth by using natural potato lumps and hand-inked variation. Face reads as mature around 30, like a small martial-arts hero, not a child.

## Shop Background Layer

Generate the background layer only for a small potato-world roadside shop. It should be a simple hand-painted back wall with a cloth awning support, a few hanging small goods silhouettes, shelves, and warm wooden planks, but no front counter and no shopkeeper. The layer should work behind separate shop body, counter, and shopkeeper sprites.

Use a perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background. Centered isolated prop layer, front-facing with slight 3/4 depth, generous padding. Keep shapes broad and animation-friendly.

## Shop Body Layer

Generate the shop body layer only for a small potato-world roadside shop: chunky wooden stall frame, curved cloth roof canopy, side posts, little blank signboard shape, simple crates attached to the frame. No front counter and no shopkeeper. It should layer in front of a separate back wall and behind a separate sales counter.

Use a perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background. Centered isolated shop structure, front-facing slight 3/4 view, generous padding. Leave the middle opening readable so a shopkeeper layer can sit inside. Broad pieces suitable for parallax/layered animation.

## Shop Front Counter Layer

Generate the front counter layer only for a small potato-world shop. A chunky wooden sales counter with rounded plank shapes, simple display goods such as a few potion bottles, potato snacks, price-tag shapes with no text, and a small tray. No shopkeeper and no full shop structure behind it.

Use a perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background. Centered isolated horizontal counter, front-facing with slight 3/4 top visibility, generous padding. It should sit in front of the shop body and shopkeeper in a layered composition.

## Shopkeeper Layer

Generate the shopkeeper layer only for a small potato-world shop. A friendly middle-aged potato merchant bust/upper-body sprite that can stand behind a separate counter: round potato head/body, little merchant cap, curled moustache or thick eyebrows, small spectacles optional, warm sly smile, simple apron collar. No full shop, no counter, no arms reaching outside the silhouette.

Use a perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background. Centered isolated shopkeeper bust, front-facing slight 3/4 view, generous padding. The shopkeeper should fit behind the separate front counter and inside the shop body opening. Friendly merchant personality, not villainous or scary.
